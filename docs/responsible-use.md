# Responsible use

EchoTwin recreates how a person writes and sounds. That is powerful and easy to abuse, so:

* **One acknowledgement, no signing.** Before any voice is synthesized you tick one box:
  the voice is yours, or you are entitled to recreate it (for example a relative's, with
  their family's permission), you will not use it to deceive anyone, and you will say when
  speech is AI-generated. Your own voice is the simplest case.
* **No script needed.** Upload any clear recording (10-60 s is plenty). It is transcribed
  automatically; a script is only an optional helper.
* **Public recordings are not permission.** Do not clone living or deceased public figures
  to impersonate them or to put words in their mouth. Legitimate sources are your own
  voice, family members with permission, volunteers who agreed, and recordings explicitly
  released for this kind of reuse (open-licensed or public-domain corpora such as Common
  Voice or LibriVox readings, which are CC0/public domain).
* **More text makes a better persona.** Style is learned from what the person wrote or said:
  hundreds of messages or several transcripts of their speaking give clearly better
  imitations than a paragraph. Voice quality depends on the recording, not on text.
* **Label the output** as AI-generated whenever you share it.
